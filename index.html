Eight traits, not one label.

Questions

Can an online test tell me if I'm a narcissist?

No. Only a licensed mental health professional can diagnose Narcissistic Personality Disorder. This test shows which traits came through in your answers and how far those answers can be trusted.

Isn't it easy to lie on these tests?

On short quizzes, yes. This one makes it harder and more visible, with scenarios that have no obvious right answer, questions about recent behavior, built-in checks, and optional ratings from someone who knows you. A determined person can still fake it. See the method.

Are my answers stored or shared?

They stay in your browser, on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no server receiving your answers. Clearing your browser data erases them.

If I'm worried I'm a narcissist, does that mean I'm not?

Not necessarily. That's a popular myth. People with narcissistic traits can be self-aware, especially those with more vulnerable traits. Worrying about your impact is a good sign, but it isn't proof either way.

Someone keeps calling me a narcissist. Are they right?

Maybe, partly, or not at all. The word can point at a real pattern, stand in for “you hurt me,” or be used as a weapon. The guide covers how to tell the difference.

Can I use this to find out if my partner or parent is a narcissist?

No. It only works for looking at yourself. To make sense of someone in your life, start with the guide. If you feel unsafe, the National Domestic Violence Hotline is free and confidential.
